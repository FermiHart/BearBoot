The non-claim is part of the protocol

Integrity is not authenticity.

CRC-64/XZ detects accidental corruption and casual tampering. A producer controlling the bytes can recompute it. BBP never promotes a valid checksum into a trusted identity or semantic truth.

MAPPED EXTENTThe consumer must supply readable bounds before the parser can safely inspect producer memory.
IMMUTABLE THROUGH USEValidation and consumption must not race producer-controlled mutation; copy when the lifetime is uncertain.
EXTERNAL AUTHORITYSecure Boot, signatures, TPM policy and platform-specific semantics live above CRC framing.
